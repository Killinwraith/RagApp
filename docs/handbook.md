The Daily Grind Coffee Shop Employee Handbook

The Daily Grind Coffee Shop is a neighborhood coffee shop focused on serving high-quality coffee, fresh pastries, and light meals in a welcoming environment. Our mission is to provide excellent customer service, maintain consistent product quality, and create a comfortable space where customers can relax, work, and socialize.

The coffee shop operates Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM and Saturday and Sunday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Employees must arrive at least 15 minutes before their scheduled shift to prepare their workstation. Store managers are responsible for ensuring that opening and closing procedures are completed on time.

Employees must wear a clean company-branded shirt, dark trousers, and closed-toe shoes while working. Long hair must be tied back when preparing food or beverages. Clothing must be clean and free from offensive images or language. Jewelry should be minimal to maintain food safety standards.

Employees are expected to arrive on time for every scheduled shift. If an employee expects to be late, they must notify the shift supervisor at least 30 minutes before their shift begins. Repeated lateness may result in a formal performance discussion. Employees who cannot attend work due to illness must notify management as early as possible.

Employees working shifts longer than five hours are entitled to breaks according to applicable employment legislation. Break schedules are coordinated by the shift supervisor to ensure that customer service is not interrupted. Employees must clock out when required by company policy and return promptly after their scheduled breaks.

Every customer should be greeted with a friendly welcome when entering the coffee shop. Employees should listen carefully to customer requests, communicate clearly, and maintain a positive attitude. Complaints should be handled respectfully and professionally. If an issue cannot be resolved immediately, the shift supervisor should be contacted.

Espresso beverages must be prepared using the shop's standard recipes. A standard espresso shot uses approximately 18 grams of ground coffee and produces approximately 36 grams of espresso. Baristas must follow the approved extraction and preparation procedures to maintain consistent flavor and quality.

Milk must be stored in refrigerated conditions at temperatures specified by food safety requirements. Opened milk containers must be dated and handled according to the shop's food safety procedures. Milk should never be returned to its original container after being poured into a steaming pitcher. Alternative milk products must be stored separately where appropriate to prevent cross-contact.

Employees must wash their hands before handling food, after using the restroom, after handling waste, and whenever contamination may have occurred. Food preparation surfaces must be cleaned and sanitized regularly. Employees must never handle ready-to-eat food with unwashed hands or contaminated utensils.

The coffee shop offers products that may contain common allergens, including milk, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, wheat, and soy. Employees must never guess whether a product contains an allergen. When customers ask about ingredients or allergen risks, employees must consult the official ingredient and allergen information. Any uncertainty must be escalated to the manager.

Employees must use the approved point-of-sale system to process all customer transactions. Every sale must be recorded accurately. Cash payments must be counted carefully, and change must be returned correctly. Employees must never share their register login credentials or use the cash register for personal transactions.

Customers may request a refund or replacement if they receive an incorrect or defective product. Employees should apologize and attempt to resolve simple issues promptly. Refunds must be processed through the point-of-sale system according to company procedures. Larger refunds or unusual requests require manager approval.

All active employees receive a 30% discount on regular-priced food and beverages during their scheduled shifts. The discount cannot be transferred to friends or family members. Employees must apply their discount through their own employee account. Discounts cannot be combined with other promotional offers unless management explicitly permits it.

Employees must keep walkways clear, clean spills immediately, and use appropriate warning signs when floors are wet. Electrical equipment must be handled with care and kept away from water. Any injury, equipment malfunction, or unsafe working condition must be reported to the shift supervisor immediately.

Coffee machines, grinders, refrigerators, ovens, and other equipment must be maintained according to the manufacturer's instructions and the shop's maintenance schedule. Employees are responsible for routine cleaning of their assigned equipment. Only authorized personnel may perform repairs or adjustments that require technical expertise.

Each employee is responsible for maintaining a clean workstation throughout their shift. Tables must be wiped after customer use, counters must remain free of unnecessary clutter, and garbage must be removed before bins become full. A complete cleaning checklist must be completed at the end of each shift.

Employees must monitor inventory levels of coffee beans, milk, syrups, cups, lids, pastries, and other supplies. Low-stock items must be recorded in the inventory log and communicated to the shift supervisor. Employees must follow the first-in, first-out method when rotating perishable inventory.

Employees should remain calm and professional when dealing with dissatisfied customers. Staff members must listen without interrupting, acknowledge the customer's concern, and offer an appropriate solution within their authority. Serious complaints, safety concerns, or requests involving significant compensation must be escalated to the store manager.

All employees must treat coworkers, managers, suppliers, and customers with respect. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, intimidation, and aggressive behavior are not tolerated. Employees are expected to communicate professionally and contribute to a positive working environment.

Personal mobile phone usage is not permitted while employees are actively serving customers or preparing food and beverages. Phones may be used during authorized breaks in designated areas. Employees must not photograph customers, coworkers, or confidential business information without appropriate permission.

Employees must not disclose confidential business information, customer information, internal financial details, or private employee matters on social media. Employees should not present personal opinions as official statements from The Daily Grind Coffee Shop. Only authorized representatives may publish official business announcements.

Items discovered inside the coffee shop must be handed to the shift supervisor and recorded in the lost-and-found log. Employees must not keep items belonging to customers. Customers claiming lost property should provide a reasonable description of the item before it is returned.

The opening employee must unlock the premises, disable the security system using their authorized credentials, switch on approved equipment, inspect the shop for safety concerns, prepare the espresso station, check refrigerated products, and ensure that the customer area is clean. The opening checklist must be completed before serving customers.

At closing time, employees must stop accepting orders according to the posted closing schedule, clean and sanitize preparation areas, store food safely, empty designated waste containers, clean coffee equipment, secure cash according to company procedures, and ensure that all doors and windows are locked. The closing supervisor must complete the final security checklist.

In an emergency involving fire, serious injury, or immediate danger, employees must prioritize personal safety and customer safety. Emergency services should be contacted when necessary. Employees must follow evacuation instructions, avoid taking unnecessary risks, and notify management as soon as it is safe to do so. Emergency exits must remain accessible at all times.
